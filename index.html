The Shrinking Perimeter

Between 2006 and 2010, official security assessments cited over 180 districts as experiencing varying degrees of Naxalite activity. Operational consolidation, road-building programs, and security post placement have geographically compressed the movement's primary presence into a small subset of core forest sectors.
